13.2 — Explore a New Sensor

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. This lesson asks you to research a sensor on your own that you haven't used in class. What's one sensor you're curious about that we haven't covered this year?
  2. Teaching a concept to someone else is one of the best ways to find out if you actually understand it. Why do you think that's true?
  3. What would you need to figure out about a new sensor before you could explain it clearly to a classmate — what questions would you need answered first?
  4. How is researching a new sensor on your own different from following a lesson someone else already wrote for you?

Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Independent research
  • Peer teaching

Key idea: This lesson flips the usual direction — instead of following a lesson, you research a new sensor Tinkercad supports that the course hasn't covered, figure out how to wire and code it yourself, and then teach it to your classmates. This is deliberately open-ended: the skill being tested isn't just "can you use a new sensor," it's "can you teach yourself something new and then explain it clearly to someone else," which is exactly what real engineers do constantly on the job.

Assignment
