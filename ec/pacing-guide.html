Year-Long Pacing Guide

A 36-week plan (two 18-week semesters). Each unit ends with a Unit Test and a Google Site Check on the same day. Weeks are a target — build days sometimes run long, so expect small shifts.

Rebuilt from actual progress, at a fixed 2 assignments/week from the week of Sept 21 on (that week is 2.2 only). At this pace the calendar runs out at Week 36 (May 17) partway through Unit 11 (11.1–11.2) — Units 12–18 (Sensors, Advanced Arduino, Strings, Data Structures, Guess the Word, and the Final Exam) have no scheduled weeks at all this year. See the note after Week 36 below.
