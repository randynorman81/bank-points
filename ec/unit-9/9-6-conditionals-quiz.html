Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Review: booleans, if/else, comparison operators, logical operators, floating point rounding

Key idea: This quiz covers the whole Conditionals module: booleans, if/else and Python's indentation rules, the six comparison operators, the and/or/not logical operators, and floating point rounding. Conditionals are the single most-used building block in programming — almost every interesting program makes a decision somewhere — so being solid here pays off directly in the Looping and Functions modules right after this, and again when you get to Sensors, where an Arduino has to decide what to do based on what a sensor reads.
