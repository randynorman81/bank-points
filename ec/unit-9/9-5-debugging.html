Notes

📄 Notes: Debugging (everything you need for the lesson)

Look up these definitions:

  • Debugging
  • Serial Monitor
  • Serial.print()
  • Serial.println()
  • Bug

Key idea: Debugging is finding and fixing problems in your code or circuit. The Serial Monitor lets your Arduino send values back to your screen as it runs, so you can see what it is actually doing. Following the same step-by-step process every time makes bugs much easier to find.
