3.11 — V1 and V2 Alarm System

Independent build, two periods. Your first V1/V2 iteration of the year — a preview of the Scrum framework coming in Unit 4.

Extra Credit Quiz

Today we'll take the 3.11 Quiz — 10 questions for extra credit. The first 5 cover earlier material; the last 5 cover this unit.

Notes

No new vocabulary today.

What you're building: an alarm using any sensor (or combination) from this unit — photoresistor (simulation), HC-SR04, PIR, or DHT11 — plus your buzzer. There's no "best" sensor choice; a simple, reliable single-sensor alarm beats an ambitious one that doesn't work. V1 requirements: a working alarm that detects a condition and triggers the buzzer, using if/else correctly, fully debugged. V2 requirements: a genuine, specific improvement on V1 — not cosmetic. Acceptable examples: a second sensor for combined detection, Serial Monitor logging of every trigger, a reset mechanism, or the loop-based averaging technique from 3.6 to reduce false triggers. Not a day for AI to generate your solution — using it to debug a specific error is fine.

Assignments

  • 3.11 — V1 and V2 Alarm System. Choose your sensor(s), plan V1, wire it, then write and debug it independently using the methodology from 3.10. Once V1 works reliably, plan and build a specific V2 improvement. Submit your fully annotated V1 code, your fully annotated V2 code, a photo of each, and a written reflection on what V2 improved and why you chose it.
  • Add to Page 2.3 — Unit 3 on your Google Site: your V1 alarm photo, your V2 alarm photo, 2–3 sentences on what V1 did and what V2 improved, and one thing you'd build next. (Your case connection paragraph from Lesson 3.8 should already be on this page.)

Directions

  1. Choose your sensor(s) and plan your V1 alarm.
  2. Wire, write, and debug V1 independently.
  3. Once V1 works reliably, plan a genuine V2 improvement.
  4. Build and debug V2.
  5. Submit your annotated V1 and V2 code, photos, and reflection in Google Classroom.
  6. Update Page 2.3 on your Google Site.
