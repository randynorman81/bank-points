Unit 3: Sensors and Reactive Programs

Your programs start reacting to the physical world instead of just running on a fixed schedule. You'll learn if/else and loops, work through four different sensors (light, distance, motion, temperature), study two real cases about trusting sensor and input data (the Jeep Cherokee hack, the Oldsmar water plant attack), and close with your first V1/V2 independent build — a working alarm system.
