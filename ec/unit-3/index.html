Unit 3: Arduino Foundations II

You've already built every one of these in TinkerCAD. Now you build them again with a real Arduino Uno, real components, and a multimeter — and each lesson adds one genuinely new twist that simulation couldn't give you: measuring real current against your calculations, a second alternating LED, a potentiometer controlling blink speed and brightness live, a pedestrian button that interrupts your traffic light sequence, and timing your own board's real compile cycle. It closes with a more ambitious independent build than Unit 2's: a real traffic light that also responds to a real button.
