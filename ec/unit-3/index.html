Unit 3: Outputs: Light, Sound, and Motion

Unit 2 taught you to blink an LED. Now you make things light up in color, play music, swing, spin, and count — all in Tinkercad Circuits, so you can build, test, and share from any Chromebook. You will wire an RGB LED, a buzzer, a servo, a motor with a transistor, and a 7-segment display, then connect a dial to drive them, organize your code with functions and arrays, learn to debug like an engineer, and finish with a machine of your own design.
