3.12 — Case: Toyota Unintended Acceleration

Intro

Ask AI these questions; look over the answers.

  1. What happened with Toyota's unintended acceleration problem between 2009 and 2011? What caused it?
  2. What is an electronic throttle control system and how is it different from a traditional mechanical gas pedal? Explain it to a middle schooler.
  3. What does "fail-safe" mean in engineering? Create a simple diagram or visual showing what a fail-safe response looks like versus a system with no fail-safe.
  4. Connect a car's computer system malfunctioning at high speed to a movie or TV show I might know — where has this kind of disaster shown up in fiction?
  5. How does this case show up in our society today — are there other examples of car companies dealing with software or electronics problems in their vehicles?
  6. When a car's onboard computer system causes a fatal accident, what usually happens to the company afterward? Has any company ever faced criminal charges for this?
