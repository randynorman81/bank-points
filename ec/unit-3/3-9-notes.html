The Idea Menu

Part 1: Plan

Pick one from the menu, or invent your own. Whatever you pick, you should be able to say it in one sentence: "When ___ happens, my machine does ___."

Plan before you build: fill in the plan table in your assignment first: name, one-sentence description, inputs, three outputs, your 3 function names and jobs, and what your array holds.
03

Plan Your Pins

Part 2, Step 3

Some parts share hidden hardware inside the Arduino called timers. If two parts need the same timer, they fight, and things act strangely: a dim LED that won't dim, or a servo that twitches. Plan your pins here and the checker flags the known fights.

The two conflicts from your assignment

  • tone() uses the same timer as PWM on pins 3 and 11.
  • The Servo library uses the timer for PWM on pins 9 and 10.

If two parts act strangely together, try moving one to a different pin.

Other pin rules

  • analogWrite() (dimming, motor speed) needs a ~ pin: 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11.
  • A dial needs an analog pin: A0–A5.
  • Pins 0 and 1 are used by the Serial Monitor, so avoid them.
  • Each pin does one job.
04

Build One Piece at a Time, Then Test

Parts 2 + 3

Pros never wire the whole machine and hope. They get one part working alone, save it, then add the next. When something breaks, it's always the piece they just added.

Building

  1. Wire and test one part before adding the next (for example, get the motor working alone, then add the display).
  2. After each part works, take a quick screenshot in case you need to go back.
  3. Use Serial.println to print values when something isn't working (3.8's method).

Your test log

Test at least 4 things. For each: what you tested, what you expected, what actually happened. Example (not from your machine):

TestedExpectedHappened
Turned the dial all the way right7-segment shows 9Showed 8; fixed the map() top value
Turning it in: the plan table, the test log, commented code pasted in, and a screenshot of your circuit running (with the Serial Monitor open if it prints). Then the three reflection questions.
