3.9 — Unit Build: Your Own Machine

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Look back at everything you built this unit. Which part was your favorite, and what would you like to combine it with?
  2. A good design starts with a sentence: 'When ___ happens, my machine does ___.' Write one for an idea you have.
  3. Building one piece at a time and testing is safer than building everything and hoping. Why?
  4. How will you know your machine is finished? Name two things you can test.

Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Requirements
  • Prototype
  • Pin conflict
  • Documentation

Key idea: This is your unit build: design and build your own machine in Tinkercad. It needs at least one input, at least three different kinds of output (LED or RGB LED, buzzer, servo, motor, 7-segment display), at least three custom functions, at least one array, Serial output for debugging, and comments on every section. Add one part at a time and test after each. Watch for pin conflicts: tone() takes over PWM on pins 3 and 11, and the Servo library takes over PWM on pins 9 and 10.
