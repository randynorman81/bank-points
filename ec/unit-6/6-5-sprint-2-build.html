6.5 — Sprint 2 Build

Four more full periods, same structure as Sprint 1 (10-min standup, then build) — but this time you're building forward from a working foundation, not starting from a blank slate. Build on your Sprint 1 code rather than rewriting it from scratch unless you have a real, considered reason to.

What you're building

The genuinely new element from your Lesson 6.4 Sprint 2 plan, integrated with your working Sprint 1 MVP. By the end of this lesson: a complete project combining Sprint 1's MVP with Sprint 2's new addition; fully annotated code; a photo or video of the finished build; and Sprint 1's original functionality still working, not broken during integration.

If integrating your Sprint 2 addition with your Sprint 1 foundation is the sticking point, that's the same integration challenge from Lessons 4.6 and 5.7 — combine incrementally, test after each addition. If Sprint 2 isn't fully working by the end of this lesson, that's honest material for Lesson 6.6's retrospective.

Assignments

  • 6.5 — Sprint 2 Build. Submit your fully annotated, complete combined system code (Sprint 1 MVP + Sprint 2 addition), along with photos or video documenting your build progress. One submission covering all 4 periods.
  • Add Sprint 2 progress photos to Page 2.6 — Unit 6 on your Google Site incrementally throughout the 4 periods.

What do you actually need to do and turn in?

Build your Sprint 2 addition on top of your working Sprint 1 MVP. Turn in fully annotated, complete combined code and a photo or video, as one submission covering all 4 periods.

What do you need to know to complete this?

No new content today. You'll need your working Sprint 1 code and your Sprint 2 plan from 6.4; combining components incrementally is the same integration approach from Lessons 4.6 and 5.7.

Where do you turn it in?

Google Classroom for your code and photo/video; progress photos also go on your Google Site, added incrementally throughout the 4 periods.

Solo, partners, or group?

Partners — with your assigned project partner.

How many points is this worth?

100 points — this is a 4-period build.

Extra credit options

Directions

In paragraph form: Start each period with a 10-minute standup, same structure as Sprint 1. Build the genuinely new element from your Lesson 6.4 Sprint 2 plan, integrated with your working Sprint 1 MVP — build on your existing code rather than rewriting from scratch unless you have a real, considered reason to. Combine incrementally and test after each addition, the same integration approach from Lessons 4.6 and 5.7. By the end you should have a complete project combining Sprint 1's MVP with Sprint 2's new addition, fully annotated code, a photo or video of the finished build, and Sprint 1's original functionality still working. Add progress photos to Page 2.6 on your Google Site as you go.

Step by step:

  1. Start each of the 4 periods with a 10-minute standup.
  2. Build the new Sprint 2 element on top of your Sprint 1 code.
  3. Integrate and test incrementally, confirming Sprint 1's functionality still works.
  4. Submit your fully annotated combined code and media as one submission.
  5. Add progress photos to Page 2.6 on your Google Site incrementally.
