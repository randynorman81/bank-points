6.4 — Sprint 1 Retrospective

Same three-question format as Lesson 4.9, now applied to a 4-period sprint — give it real depth.

Intro

Ask AI these questions; look over the answers.

  1. Why might a retrospective be more valuable after a long, complex sprint like the one you just finished, compared to a shorter, simpler build?
  2. Explain to a middle schooler why "what would you do differently" is a more useful question than just "did it work or not?"
  3. Create a simple template for planning a second phase of work based on lessons learned from a first phase.
  4. How should a team decide what to prioritize for a second version, when there are likely many possible improvements to choose from?
  5. Connect today's reflection to the Nest Thermostat story from Lesson 6.1 — what kind of retrospective do you imagine the Nest team had after their V1?
  6. Why might it be tempting to skip writing an honest retrospective when you're excited to just start building the next phase?

Notes

No new vocabulary today — update the Comfort Rating on your existing sprint, retrospective, and backlog entries instead of adding new rows.

Same three questions as 4.9: what went well (specific moments/decisions, not "it went fine")? What didn't go well (be honest)? What would you do differently (connects directly to the above)? This sprint was 4 full periods of independent work — give your reflection depth to match. Sprint 2 should represent genuine new progress, not more time finishing what Sprint 1 was already supposed to do — if your MVP isn't fully done, Sprint 2 should finish that core functionality AND add something genuinely new, the same way V2 added the servo on top of V1's already-working logic in Unit 5.

Assignments

  • 6.4 — Sprint 1 Retrospective + Sprint 2 Plan. Part 1: a genuine, specific retrospective on Sprint 1 — what went well, what didn't, what you'd do differently, with depth appropriate to a 4-period sprint. Part 2: your Sprint 2 plan — what genuinely new element you'll add beyond the MVP, what (if anything) still needs finishing from Sprint 1, and what success looks like for Sprint 2 specifically. Format: Doc or Slides.
