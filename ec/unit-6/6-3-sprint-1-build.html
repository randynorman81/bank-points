6.3 — Sprint 1 Build

Four full periods, the longest continuous build of the course — independent design and build toward your own Design Brief's MVP goal. No new content today; this is entirely applying what you already know. Standup (10 min: what you accomplished last period, today's specific goal, any blockers) starts every period, followed by build time — repeated identically across all 4 periods.

What you're building

Your own Sprint 1 (MVP), exactly as described in your approved Design Brief. By the end of this lesson you should have: a working MVP matching (or a documented, justified deviation from) your Sprint 1 goal; fully annotated code; and at least one photo or video of your build.

If you haven't reached a working MVP by the end of this lesson, that's honest, real material for your Lesson 6.4 retrospective — not something to hide or overstate.

Assignments

  • 6.3 — Sprint 1 Build (MVP). Submit your fully annotated Sprint 1 code, matching (or documenting a justified deviation from) your approved Design Brief's MVP goal, along with photos or video documenting your build progress. This is one submission covering all 4 periods.
  • Add Sprint 1 progress photos to Page 2.6 — Unit 6 on your Google Site incrementally throughout the 4 periods, not saved until the end.
