2.6 — Timing and Sequences

Two-period lesson, new wiring — three LEDs today (red, yellow, green). This directly sets up your Unit 2 build in Lesson 2.9.

Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Sequence
  • Blocking code
  • State
  • Timing logic

Key idea: delay() pauses your entire program — nothing else can run while it's counting down. This is called blocking code, and it's fine for everything in this unit, but it becomes a real limitation starting in Unit 3 (more on that with interrupts). Wiring three LEDs: each needs its own resistor and its own digital pin, but they can all share one ground connection back to GND. A sequence is just digitalWrite() + delay() repeated in a deliberate order across multiple pins.
