Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Source current
  • Sink current
  • Pull-up resistor
  • Floating pin
  • Ohm's Law

Key idea: a pin sources current when it pushes current out (digitalWrite HIGH), and sinks current when current flows into it. A button wired with no defined HIGH/LOW when unpressed is floating — it picks up random noise and gives unreliable readings. A pull-up resistor fixes this by connecting the pin to 5V by default (the Uno has a built-in one via pinMode(pin, INPUT_PULLUP)). Ohm's Law: V = IR (voltage = current × resistance) — rearrange to R = V/I to find the resistor an LED circuit needs. The Uno's pins max out around 40mA — exceeding that can damage the pin or board, which is exactly why every LED gets a resistor.
