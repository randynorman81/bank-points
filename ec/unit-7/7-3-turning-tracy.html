7.3 — Turning Tracy

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Tracy can turn left() or right(), but only by fixed 90-degree steps in this lesson. What shapes can you draw using only forward moves and 90-degree turns?
  2. Why do you think left() and right() turn a fixed amount instead of asking you to type in a direction like "turn to face north"?
  3. If you wanted Tracy to draw a square, how many turns would you need, and how many degrees each?
  4. Predict what would happen if you called right() twice in a row without moving forward in between.

Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • left() / right()
  • Heading (direction)

Key idea: Turning commands change Tracy's heading — the direction she's currently facing — without moving her position. In this lesson, left() and right() turn her by a fixed 90 degrees, which is enough to draw shapes made of straight, perpendicular lines like squares and staircases. Combining forward moves with turns is how every Tracy drawing gets built, one small, exact step at a time.
