Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • WiFi
  • Bluetooth
  • BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy)
  • Zigbee
  • Z-Wave

Key idea: WiFi — high speed, moderate range, higher power draw. Bluetooth — connects nearby devices directly (headphones, speakers). BLE — a separate, low-power version of Bluetooth built for devices running months/years on a small battery (fitness trackers, key fobs) — sends less data, less often, in exchange for battery life. Zigbee and Z-Wave — low-power mesh technologies where devices relay data to each other, letting a smart home with many devices cover more area reliably than any one device's direct range. No technology is universally best — each is a tradeoff between range, speed, power, and cost. Wired connections trade convenience for reliability and are harder to intercept than wireless — a recurring theme since Unit 5's RFID wireless-interception cases.
