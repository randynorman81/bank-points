Unit 7: IoT Concepts

The course shifts from individual embedded systems to networked, internet-connected ones — the "IoT" in Embedded Computing and IoT. Mostly discussion-heavy, with one hands-on lesson (connecting a real Arduino to Arduino Cloud), this unit consolidates I2C and SPI side by side, introduces wireless networking and cloud architecture, and closes with two cases on IoT security failures at scale — the Mirai Botnet and Ring camera hacks.
