Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • IoT (Internet of Things)
  • Smart device
  • Device ecosystem
  • Data exchange
  • Connectivity

Key idea: IoT means physical, embedded devices connected to a network and each other — not just "anything involving computers." Your traffic light (Unit 2), alarm (Unit 3), PIN entry system (Unit 4), and RFID lock (Unit 5) were all standalone embedded systems — fully functional but not IoT, because none had connectivity. Lesson 7.6's Arduino Cloud lab will be this course's first genuinely connected build. Device ecosystems create convenience (everything works together) but also risk (one vulnerability in the central service can affect every connected device at once) — this sets up 7.5 and 7.7's cases directly.
