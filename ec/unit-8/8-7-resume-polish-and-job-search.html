You're not starting from scratch — you built this resume back in Unit 1, Lesson 1.6. Today you're leveling it up to reflect a full year of real, demonstrable skill.

Extra Credit Quiz

Today we'll take the 8.7 Quiz — 10 questions for extra credit. The first 5 cover earlier material; the last 5 cover this unit.

Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Skill matching
  • Action verb
  • Entry-level position

Your resume should now reflect every major project (traffic light, alarm, PIN entry, RFID lock, Unit 6 design project, this capstone) and specific technical skills (Arduino programming, sensor integration, debugging methodology, Scrum/sprint planning). Strong language is specific ("Built an RFID-based access control system integrating a servo motor and LCD display," not "Did a project with sensors"), uses action verbs (built, designed, debugged, integrated), and is honest — real specificity is more credible than exaggeration. Skill matching means directly comparing a real job posting's requirements against your actual skills, honestly noting gaps as well as matches.
