Notes

SkillsUSA's full arc: introduced in Unit 1, revisited through Unit 6's Nest story and Demo Day, and today — the most direct connection yet: could your actual capstone become a real competitive entry? Relevant categories: Electronics Technology (hands-on circuit skill demonstrations) and Engineering Technology/Design (project-based design, a strong fit for a capstone like yours). Not every capstone becomes a real entry, and that's completely fine — today's value is genuine consideration, not a requirement to compete.
