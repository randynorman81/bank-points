8.6 — Final Presentation

Two full periods — the course's final live presentation event. Set your project up and ready to demonstrate at the start of class.

Presenting

Your presentation should cover, in order: (1) what your capstone does — a brief live demonstration; (2) why you made your key design choices; (3) what changed between Sprint 1 and Sprint 2, and why; (4) specifically how skills from at least 2 other units made this capstone possible; (5) one honest limitation or thing you'd improve with more time.

Individual Reflection

After presentations, write an individual reflection (not a pair submission):

  • Looking back at your very first lessons in Unit 1, how would you describe your growth to yourself from back then?
  • What's the single most valuable skill or concept from this entire course, and why?
  • What are you most proud of from your capstone specifically?
  • What's one thing about embedded computing or IoT you're curious to learn more about, beyond this course?

Assignments

  • 8.6 — Final Presentation Individual Reflection. Submit your individual reflection (see above) — the only individual, non-pair submission this unit.
  • Complete Page 2.8 — Unit 8 on your Google Site: your final capstone photo or video, and "what I'd build next." This completes the entire My Builds section — the full 8-page set built across the whole year.
  • Fill out your developer log on your Google Site every class period (graded at the end of the unit; timestamps checked).
