8.2 — Sprint 1 Build

Directions

  1. Start each of the 6 periods with a 10-minute standup.
  2. Build toward your approved Capstone Proposal's Sprint 1 goal.
  3. Document your build with photos or video showing genuine integration breadth.
  4. Submit your fully annotated code and media as one combined submission.
  5. Add progress photos to Page 2.8 on your Google Site incrementally.

Six full periods — the longest continuous build of the entire course. Same rhythm as Unit 6's sprints: 10-min standup (what you accomplished, today's specific goal, blockers), then build time — repeated identically across all 6 periods.

What you're building

Your own Sprint 1 goal, exactly as described in your approved Capstone Proposal. By the end of this lesson you should have: a working version of your Sprint 1 goal, matching (or a documented, justified deviation from) your proposal; annotated code demonstrating genuine integration breadth across multiple units' skills; and at least one photo or video of your build.

If you haven't reached your Sprint 1 goal by the end of this lesson, that's honest, real material for your Lesson 8.3 retrospective — there's no later unit to recover in, so course-correct early if your scope turns out to be unrealistic.

Assignments

  • 8.2 — Sprint 1 Build. Submit your fully annotated Sprint 1 code, matching (or documenting a justified deviation from) your approved Capstone Proposal's Sprint 1 goal, along with photos or video documenting your build progress. One submission covering all 6 periods.
  • Add Sprint 1 progress photos to Page 2.8 — Unit 8 on your Google Site incrementally throughout the 6 periods.

How much is it worth?

100 points — this is a 6-period build, the longest continuous build of the entire course.

Partners? Group?

Partners - you will work with your assigned project partner.

Where do you turn it in?

Google Classroom for your code and photo/video; progress photos also go on your Google Site, added incrementally throughout the 6 periods.
