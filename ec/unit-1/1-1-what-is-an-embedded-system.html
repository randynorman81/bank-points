1.1 — What Is an Embedded System?

Intro

Using AI, ask at least 3 of these questions:

  1. What is an embedded system? Give me three examples I would recognize from my daily life as a high school student.
  2. What is the difference between an embedded system and a regular computer like a laptop? Explain it to a middle schooler.
  3. What is a microcontroller and how is it different from a microprocessor? Create a simple diagram or visual that explains the difference.
  4. What crimes or real disasters have happened because an embedded system failed or was hacked? Give one specific example, explain what went wrong technically.
  5. Connect the concept of embedded systems to a movie, TV show, or video game. How does that story relate to what embedded systems actually do in real life?
  6. What jobs exist for people who build embedded systems? What do they earn and what do they study?
