1.7 — AI in Embedded Computing and IoT

Intro

Ask AI these questions; look over the answers.

  1. What is the difference between edge AI and cloud AI? Give me a real example of each.
  2. Explain to a middle schooler how a smart camera can recognize a face without sending the video anywhere over the internet.
  3. Create a simple diagram showing the difference between a device that processes AI on-device versus a device that sends data to the cloud for AI processing.
  4. Connect AI-powered embedded devices to a movie or TV show I might know — where has AI in a physical device played a major role in the story?
  5. What real consumer products today use AI inside an embedded system? List at least three and explain what the AI actually does in each one.
  6. If an AI-powered medical device makes a wrong decision and harms a patient, who should be responsible — the company that built the AI, the company that built the device, or the doctor who used it?
