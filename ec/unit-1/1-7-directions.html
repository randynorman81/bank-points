1.7 — AI Product Investigation Team

Real companies researching a competitor's product almost never send one person to figure it out alone — they send a small team, each person covering a different angle, then combine what they found. You're doing the same thing today in a 3-person Investigation Team.

Steps

  1. Form a team of 3 and assign roles: Technical Lead, Impact Analyst, Case Presenter.
  2. Pick an AI-powered embedded product to investigate (or pitch your own to Mr. Norman).
  3. Create one shared Google Doc or Slides deck, shared with all three teammates.
  4. Technical Lead writes the Technical Analysis section.
  5. Impact Analyst writes the Real-World Impact section.
  6. Case Presenter writes the Takeaway, adds the Sources section, and finishes assembling the file.
  7. Case Presenter presents the finished product to the class in under 2 minutes.
  8. One team member submits the shared link in Google Classroom.

Roles

Each role has a specific, checkable job.

Role 1 — Technical Lead
Figures out how the product's AI actually works. Is it Edge AI, Cloud AI, or a hybrid of both? What embedded hardware or sensors is it probably using? Where does Machine Learning fit in? Writes the Technical Analysis section.
Role 2 — Impact Analyst
Figures out the real-world side. What specific problem does this product solve, who actually uses it, and what real controversies, failures, or privacy concerns has it faced, if any? Writes the Real-World Impact section.
Role 3 — Case Presenter
Combines Roles 1 and 2's research into the Takeaway section, assembles the final Doc or Slides, and presents it to the class in under 2 minutes. Every team member should still be ready to answer a question about their own role's research if called on.
This is a shared team grade. If your team's submission is missing any one role's required piece, the whole team's grade drops 15 points — not just that person's share. Choose teammates who will actually do their part, and hold each other to it.

Grading

100 points. Take the 1.7 Quiz for extra credit — 3 points for a 100, 2 for a 90 or higher, 1 just for finishing it.

Partners / Group

Groups of 3, with a role for each person (Technical Lead, Impact Analyst, Case Presenter). Everyone in the group gets the same grade, so make sure all three roles get done.

Where Do You Turn It In?

One team member submits the shared Doc or Slides link to Google Classroom under today's 1.7 assignment, before the end of the period. Double-check your sharing settings allow Mr. Norman to open it.
