1.3 — Embedded Systems All Around Us

Intro

Ask AI these questions; look over the answers.

  1. Where do embedded systems show up in vehicles, hospitals, and the criminal justice system? Give me one specific real example from each category.
  2. Explain how a pacemaker works as an embedded system — what are its inputs, what are its outputs, and what happens if it fails? Explain it to a middle schooler.
  3. What jobs exist for people who design and build embedded systems and IoT devices? What do they pay at entry level and what education do they need?
  4. Connect embedded systems in vehicles to a movie or TV show I might know — where has a car's computer system played a major role in the story?
  5. What is SkillsUSA and what competitive events relate to embedded computing, electronics, or IoT? What would a student need to do to compete?
  6. If a pacemaker's embedded system fails because of a software bug, who should be legally responsible — the engineer, the company, or the doctor who implanted it? What does your AI think and why?
