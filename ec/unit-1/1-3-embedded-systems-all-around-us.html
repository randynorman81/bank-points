1.3 — Embedded Systems All Around Us

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. You probably touched five or more embedded systems before you even got to school this morning. Why do you think something so common is also something almost nobody notices?
  2. "Arduino" gets used as a nickname for an entire category of hardware, even though it's really one specific brand. Why does it matter — for a job interview or a real project — to know the difference between the general term and the actual product?
  3. An IoT device connects an everyday object to the internet. What's one everyday object you own that isn't "smart" yet — and would connecting it to the internet actually make your life better, or just add a new way for it to break?
  4. A firmware developer and an embedded systems engineer both work close to the hardware, but their jobs aren't identical. Based on their titles alone, what do you think separates writing "firmware" from engineering the whole "system"?

  1. Open the 1.3 Embedded Tech Trading Cards document (linked below) for the full assignment.

Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Arduino (the "umbrella term" and the actual microcontroller — they are two different things)
  • Raspberry Pi (the different models, but understand what makes them different from Arduinos)
  • IoT
  • Firmware developer
  • Embedded systems engineer
