1.6 — Resume + Polish Site

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. A resume has to convince a stranger you're worth interviewing in about six seconds of skimming. What's one thing about you that's genuinely impressive but might never make it onto a one-page resume — and how would you fix that?
  2. Your Google Site is basically a public resume that never expires and that a future employer, teacher, or college could find with a search. Does knowing that change what you'd choose to put on it today?
  3. Two students list the exact same class projects on their resumes, but one describes them with specific numbers and outcomes and the other just lists the project name. Why would an employer actually care about that difference?
  4. If you're applying for a job in a field that didn't exist five years ago, like embedded AI, how do you convince someone you're qualified when your resume can't point to "years of experience" yet?
