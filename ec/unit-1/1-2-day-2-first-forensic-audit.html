1.2 (Day 2) — First Forensic Audit

The full class period is spent completing our first forensic audit.

Intro

  1. What was the Therac-25 and what happened to the patients who were treated with it?
  2. What is a race condition in software? Explain it to a middle schooler using a simple example from everyday life.
  3. Create a simple diagram or visual showing how two software processes can interfere with each other and cause a race condition.
  4. Connect the idea of a safety-critical machine failing to a movie or TV show I might know — where has this kind of disaster shown up in fiction?
  5. What real medical devices today use embedded software, and what happens if that software has a bug?
  6. If a company's product kills someone because of a software bug, who usually gets blamed first — the engineer, the company, or the regulators? Why?
