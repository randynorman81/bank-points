1.5 — Smart City Simulator: You're the IoT Engineer

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. If a city could make every traffic light, streetlight, and water meter "smart," what's the very first question an engineer should ask before deploying any of it?
  2. More data usually means better decisions. Is there a point where collecting more data about people's daily habits stops being helpful and starts being a problem?
  3. A single vendor update system can patch every device in a city overnight. What's the hidden risk in giving one system that much power over every device at once?
  4. If you had to choose between a slower, safer rollout of smart technology and a faster, flashier one, which would you choose as a city engineer — and would voters agree with you?

Directions

  1. Open the Smart City Simulator (linked below).
  2. Play through all 4 decisions as Ridgefield's new Chief IoT Engineer.
  3. See which ending your choices lead to — then try it again with different choices if you want to see another one.
  4. Complete the reflection assignment below based on your ending.

You've spent this unit investigating embedded systems that already failed. Today you're on the other side of that story — making the calls before anything goes wrong.

Notes

Look up these definitions:

  • Attack surface
  • Centralized system

  • Edge computing (on-device processing)
  • Security by design

Today's Activity — Smart City Simulator

You're the newly hired Chief IoT Engineer for the town of Ridgefield. The mayor wants the town smarter this year. You'll make 4 real engineering decisions — each one trades off convenience, privacy, and security a little differently, and there's no single "correct" answer to any of them.

🏙 Play: Smart City Simulator

Assignments

  • Reflection (half page): describe the ending you got and the path of choices that led there. Would you change any of your 4 decisions knowing the outcome? Identify the one decision you think mattered most for security or privacy, and explain why, citing the specific tradeoff the simulator showed you.
