# Parent Newsletter Template

Reusable skeleton for the "Message" box on [/newsletter-admin.html](newsletter-admin.html).
Copy the plain-text block below, fill in the bracketed parts for whichever
course you're posting about, delete any section that doesn't apply that
month, and paste the result into Message. It's plain text (no markdown) —
line breaks are preserved exactly as typed on the public page.

Covers the things a parent of a high schooler actually wants from a class
update: what's being taught, what's due, how to help at home, and how to
reach you — plus a note on the sign-in-only tools so parents unfamiliar
with them aren't left guessing.

---

## The template

```
[COURSE NAME] — [Month Year] Update

WHAT WE'RE LEARNING
[1–2 plain-language sentences on the current unit/topic — assume the
reader has never taken a CS class.]

WHAT'S DUE / COMING UP
- [Assignment or project] — due [date]
- [Quiz or test] — [date]
- [Anything AP-specific: Create PT checkpoint, exam registration deadline, etc.]

HOW YOU CAN HELP AT HOME
- Ask your student to show you their Bank Points balance or their most
  recent quiz score — both are things they can pull up in seconds.
- [Anything specific to this unit — a project that benefits from a quiet
  workspace, a deadline worth a reminder, etc.]
- The class calendar (norman-calendar.netlify.app) always shows what's
  due and when — no login needed, so you can check it directly.

CLASSROOM REMINDERS
- [Device policy, late-work policy, makeup-work policy, anything you want
  reinforced this month.]

COMING UP ON THE CALENDAR
- [Guest speaker, showcase, field trip, club meeting, etc.]

A NOTE ON TOOLS
Quizzes, Exit Tickets, and Bank Points all use your student's existing
school Google account — no new usernames or passwords, and nothing to set
up on your end.

QUESTIONS?
Email me anytime: randy.norman@socialcircleschools.org
```

## Notes

- Reuse the same skeleton for IST, AP CSP, and Embedded Computing — only
  the bracketed parts change. For AP CSP specifically, "WHAT'S DUE / COMING
  UP" is the natural place for AP Exam registration/fee deadlines and
  Create Performance Task checkpoints once you have those dates.
- Sections with nothing to say that month (e.g. no classroom reminder
  needed) can just be deleted rather than left blank.
- "A NOTE ON TOOLS" rarely changes — write it once, keep it in your paste
  buffer, and only touch the top few sections each month.
