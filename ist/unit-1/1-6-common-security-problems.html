Today's Assignment: Inspect the Building

One 45-minute class period. Access this in Google Classroom. Read The Front Door first, then work the 8 cases below.

📄 Assignment Doc — Inspect the Building
TimeStepWhat You'll Do
0–10 minReadWork through The Front Door if you haven't already.
10–35 minInspectWork the 8 cases below on your own or with a partner.

For each case, name which protection is missing, working, or being tested — HTTPS, CAPTCHA, 2FA, or an OWASP-style flaw — and one sentence explaining why, using a term from today's notes.

Case 1
An online forum lets anyone post as fast as a script can submit forms, and spam bots have posted 500 ads in the last hour.
Case 2
A café's free Wi-Fi login page collects your email over a plain http:// connection with no padlock icon.
Case 3
You reuse the same password everywhere, it leaks in an unrelated data breach, and now someone can log into your school email with just that password.
Case 4
A poorly built class sign-up form runs whatever text you paste into the "name" box directly as a database command, letting anyone delete other students' entries.
Case 5
A site has a green padlock and https:// in the address bar, and still turns out to be a fake phishing page designed to steal logins.
Case 6
A shoe brand's release keeps selling out in seconds because bots are buying every pair before a real person can click "buy."
Case 7
A company gets hacked because their web app lets a stranger view another customer's private order history just by changing a number in the URL.
Case 8
You get a login code texted to your phone for an account, but you never tried to log in.
