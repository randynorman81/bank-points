1.5 — Hacking Ethics

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. If someone breaks into a company's website, finds a serious security flaw, changes nothing, and immediately emails the company to report it — have they still committed a crime? Why or why not?
  2. A company can legally pay someone to try to hack them. Does that mean hacking itself was never really the crime — only the missing permission was? What does that tell you about how the law defines "wrong"?
  3. If a hacker breaks into a hospital's system to expose a real safety flaw that could kill patients, but never asked permission first, should their good intent matter as much as the law says it doesn't?
  4. The same law that convicts a malicious hacker also technically applies to a curious student who guesses a friend's password "just to look." Is that fair — or should a rule just be a rule, no matter the scale?
