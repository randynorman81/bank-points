1.2 — OSINT (Open Source Intelligence)

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. OSINT means every piece of information you can legally find without hacking anything — it's all public. If a stranger could build a shockingly detailed profile of your daily routine using only stuff you posted yourself, is that their fault for looking, or your fault for posting it?
  2. Public registries like the sex offender registry are OSINT tools meant to protect communities. But should information that's "technically public" — like an old arrest that was later dropped — stay searchable by anyone, forever?
  3. The keylogger demo shows how easily software can secretly record everything you type, including passwords. Does learning exactly how a tool like that works make people more careful with their own accounts, or does it just create more people who know how to misuse it?
  4. Employers and colleges increasingly use OSINT to research applicants before making a decision. Is it fair to judge someone today based on a public post they made three years ago as a completely different, younger version of themselves?
