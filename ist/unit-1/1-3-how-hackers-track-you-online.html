1.3 — How Hackers Find Your Identity and Location Online (Including Phones, Again)

Finishing 1.3.12–1.3.13

Intro

Open AI and tell it that your parents are concerned about your safety because you post too much information online in your social media apps. List all the phone apps you use to share info (social media plus any other app you give info to — add all the apps on your phone).

Then tell AI to act as an FBI agent and, based on the apps you use and what you share, have it create a list of all the things you are sharing that you DO NOT know you are sharing, and give you a grade between 0–100:

  • 0% means you are 100% safe from people stealing your info or hurting you based on your digital footprint
  • 50% means you are in danger, but not immediate or threatening danger
  • 80% or above means you are in immediate danger based on what you post
