1.5 — Password Strength: How Hackers Actually Crack Passwords

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Most people think a password like P@ssw0rd! is strong because it looks complicated. Cracking tools check exactly those substitutions automatically. Why do you think "looks hard to guess" and "is actually hard to guess" aren't the same thing?
  2. If a website gets breached and your password leaks, credential stuffing means attackers will try that same password on every other site you've ever used it on. Do you know, right now, how many of your accounts share one password?
  3. MFA can still stop an attacker even after they already have your exact password. Why would having the right password not be enough to actually get in?
  4. A keylogger doesn't need to guess your password at all — it just reads what you type. If no password is "strong enough" to beat that, what actually is?
