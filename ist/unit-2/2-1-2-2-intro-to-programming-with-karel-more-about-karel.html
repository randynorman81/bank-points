2.1, 2.2 — Introduction to Karel & More About Karel

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Karel can only move forward, turn left, and pick up or put down beepers — no turning right, no diagonal moves. If a real robot could only do those same three things, what's the most complicated task you think it could actually pull off?
  2. In "Karel in the Physical World," your group has to act out Karel's exact instructions with your own bodies. What's harder: writing the code, or actually following it step-by-step without skipping ahead or guessing what comes next?
  3. Karel has to be told literally everything, one tiny step at a time, with zero assumptions. A person giving directions to a friend would never be this exact. Why does a computer need instructions a human never would?
  4. If you built a tower or pyramid by repeating the same commands over and over by hand, and then found out there was a shortcut that did it in way less code, would the shortcut version still feel like "your" program?
