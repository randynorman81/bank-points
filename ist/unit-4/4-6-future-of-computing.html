🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Today's assignment says no AI allowed — your thinking has to be 100% your own. Why might that rule matter more for a question about your own future than it would for a worksheet on ports or file types?
  2. Pick a job you might actually want. In 10 years, do you think AI will be doing that job instead of you, doing it alongside you, or barely involved at all? What makes you say that?
  3. If you could guarantee computing gets 10x more powerful by the time you graduate college, but you had to give up one piece of how computers work today to get it, what would you give up?
  4. Is it possible to genuinely predict your own future in a field that changes as fast as computing does, or is planning around today's technology already a losing bet?
