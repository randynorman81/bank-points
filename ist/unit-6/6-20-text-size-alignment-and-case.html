3.3 — Font-Size, Text-Alignment, Letter-Case

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Look at a movie poster or a big sign. What is the biggest text, and why do you think the designer made it that size? What would change if every word were the same size?
  2. Most paragraphs are left-aligned, but titles are often centered. Why do you think centering works well for short text but is hard to read in a long paragraph?
  3. Typing IN ALL CAPITALS is called shouting. When does using capital letters on a webpage help the reader, and when does it just make things harder to read?
  4. text-transform changes how the letters look without changing the words you typed. Why might that be better than retyping everything in capitals yourself?
