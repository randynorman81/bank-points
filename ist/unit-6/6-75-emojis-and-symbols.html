3.8 — Emojis and Symbols

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Think of the last text message you sent. Which emoji did you use, and what would the message have felt like without it?
  2. Symbols like the copyright sign and arrows are not pictures. They are characters, like letters. Why does that make them different from images?
  3. Emojis can look different on an iPhone, an Android phone, and a computer. Why might the same emoji not look identical everywhere?
  4. Where on a real website have you seen a symbol (a star, a check mark, an arrow) doing a job that words could do?

Notes

📄 Emojis and Symbols Notes

Emojis and symbols are characters, not pictures. The notes have a pad of hundreds of characters to copy and paste, examples, how to style them, and a practice box.

Assignments

Build an emoji and symbol page where pasted characters do the talking: at least 10 emojis, 4 different symbols, a list that starts every item with a character, big and colored symbols, and the tags and styles you already know. Pick one of 6 themes: an emoji dictionary, an emoji story, your day in emojis, a restaurant menu, a game scoreboard, or a weather report.
