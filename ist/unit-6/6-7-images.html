3.7 — Images and Favicons

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. If you stretch an image to fit a space without respecting its aspect ratio, people's faces and objects get warped and squished. Why does something so obviously wrong still happen constantly on real websites?
  2. An image tag needs a src attribute to know where the actual picture file lives: the tag itself holds no image data at all. What does that tell you about what is really happening when a website "loads slowly"?
  3. The alt text describes a picture in words. Who needs it, and what does your page lose if every picture has a poor description?
  4. You are about to add your first image to a real website. What is one image on a site you visit a lot that you think was chosen really carefully, and why do you think that?
