6.7 — Images

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. If you stretch an image to fit a space without respecting its aspect ratio, people's faces and objects get warped and squished. Why does something so obviously wrong still happen constantly on real websites?
  2. border-radius can turn any square image into a perfect circle with one line of code. Why do so many apps (profile pictures especially) insist on circular images instead of just using the original square?
  3. An image tag needs a src attribute to know where the actual picture file lives — the tag itself holds no image data at all. What does that tell you about what's really happening when a website "loads slowly"?
  4. You're about to add your first image to a real website. What's one image on a site you visit a lot that you think was chosen really carefully, and why do you think that?
