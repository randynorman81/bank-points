6.5 — Lists

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. You could type out "Step 1, Step 2, Step 3" as regular paragraph text instead of using an actual ordered list tag. What would a website lose by skipping real list tags, even if it looks the same to the eye?
  2. Why do you think HTML forces a choice between ordered (numbered) and unordered (bulleted) lists instead of just having one generic list type? What information does that choice communicate to a reader before they even read the content?
  3. Recipes almost always use numbered lists, but grocery lists almost always use bullets. Could you swap them — a numbered grocery list, a bulleted recipe — without confusing anyone?
  4. Think about instructions you've followed recently (a game guide, a recipe, assembly directions). What went wrong, if anything, when the order of steps wasn't crystal clear?
