3.5 — Google Fonts

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Name a website, app, or movie poster whose font you could recognize instantly. How does that font make you feel before you read a single word?
  2. Fancy script fonts look amazing on a title but are exhausting in a long paragraph. Why do you think that is?
  3. If a font fails to load, the browser uses a fallback family instead. Why is it smart to always give the browser a back-up plan?
  4. Why is using ten different fonts on one page almost always a bad idea? What is a good rule for how many to use?
