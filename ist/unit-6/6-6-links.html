6.6 — Links

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. A pseudo-selector like :hover only changes how a link looks when your mouse is on top of it — it does nothing to the actual page. Why would designers bother building an effect that most visitors will barely notice?
  2. The entire World Wide Web is basically just pages connected to other pages through links. If you deleted every single link from every website on Earth but kept all the pages, what would actually be left?
  3. A link that doesn't change color, underline, or react at all when you hover over it can trick people into missing it completely. Is that ever done on purpose, and should it be allowed?
  4. Internal styling (CSS written inside the page itself) can control every link on that page at once. Why is controlling a hundred links with one rule more powerful than styling each link one at a time?
