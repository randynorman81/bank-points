3.9 — Links and Clickable Images

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. The entire World Wide Web is basically just pages connected to other pages through links. If you deleted every single link from every website on Earth but kept all the pages, what would actually be left?
  2. A link that opens in a new tab keeps your page open behind it. When is that helpful to a visitor, and when might it be annoying?
  3. Most links are blue words, but a link can also be a whole heading, a paragraph, or a picture. What does a webpage gain when anything can be clickable?
  4. "Click here" tells a reader nothing about where a link goes. What could the link say instead, and why does it matter for someone using a screen reader?
