3.4 — Editing Tags, Span Tag

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. The span tag does nothing on its own: it has no default style at all. Why would web developers even bother creating a tag whose entire purpose is to do nothing until you tell it to?
  2. You could technically make text look bold using CSS instead of the <strong> or <b> tag. So why do "editing tags" like bold and italic still exist as their own separate tags?
  3. The br tag and the hr tag have no closing tag at all. Why do you think a line break and a horizontal line do not need one?
  4. Comic books use bold and italics constantly to show emotion and emphasis. Could a webpage with zero bold or italic text still hold someone's attention, or does formatting do more work than we give it credit for?
