3.2 — The "Style" Attribute + Color

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Pick a website you visit all the time. Name three things about how its text looks (color, size, alignment, capital letters). Could plain HTML alone do those things?
  2. Your 3.1 page was plain on purpose. If you could change only ONE thing about how it looks, what would it be? What do you think you would have to type to change it?
  3. Light text on a light background is almost impossible to read. Why does a page that looks fine to the person who made it sometimes look terrible to everyone else?
  4. Predict: a style has a colon, a semicolon, and quotes. If you forgot the semicolon between two styles on the same tag, what do you think the browser would do with the second one?
