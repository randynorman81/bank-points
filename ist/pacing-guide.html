Year-Long Pacing Guide

A 36-week plan (two 18-week semesters) for the whole course. Weeks are a target, not a promise — testing days, assemblies, and breaks can shift things a few days either way.

Rebuilt from actual progress, at 2 assignments/week (Unit 2's week of Sept 21 is 2.5 and 2.6 only, matching the Unit 2 calendar). Unit 1 is assumed to have taken 6 weeks rather than 3 to line up with Unit 2's real Sept 14 start — correct that if it's wrong.

Course restructured 2026-09-23: Unit 6 (Web Design – HTML) is now split into two units at Box Model, Unit 8 (Intro to CSS) was expanded from 3 to 7 lessons (selectors/specificity, hover effects, flexbox, and grid), a new Unit 9 (Bootstrap) was added, and Complete Website Project, JavaScript, and Web Dev (jQuery/CMS) all moved later to follow Bootstrap. Weeks 1–31 below (through the CSS unit) are accurate. Weeks 32 and on are unscheduled until Unit 9 (Bootstrap) has real lesson content — see the note after Week 31.
