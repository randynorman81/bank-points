Year-Long Pacing Guide

A 36-week plan (two 18-week semesters) for the whole course. Weeks are a target, not a promise — testing days, assemblies, and breaks can shift things a few days either way.

Rebuilt from actual progress, at 2 assignments/week (Unit 2's week of Sept 21 is 2.5 and 2.6 only, matching the Unit 2 calendar). Unit 1 is assumed to have taken 6 weeks rather than 3 to line up with Unit 2's real Sept 14 start — correct that if it's wrong. At this pace the year runs out May 17 partway through Unit 9 (9.1–9.8) — the rest of Unit 9, all of Unit 10, and the final review/exam have no scheduled week. See the note after Week 36 below.
