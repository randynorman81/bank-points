Getting to Your Work in CodeHS

Starting in Unit 2, your notes, examples, and assignments for Intro to Software Technology (Georgia's Intro to Software Technology state course) live in CodeHS instead of Google Classroom. Here's how to get to them.

1. Go to CodeHS

Open codehs.com in a new tab.

💻 codehs.com

2. Log in with your school Google account

  1. Click Log In in the top right.
  2. Choose Log in with Google (or Clever, if that's what shows first).
  3. Use your @socialcircleschools.org account — the same one you use for Google Classroom.

3. Join your class (first time only)

If this is your first time opening CodeHS for this class, join using your period's code below. Already enrolled? Skip to Step 4.

PeriodJoin Code
2B29DD1
3A85DD6
4A1EB80

After logging in, click Join a New Class (or find it from your account/courses page) and enter your period's code.

4. Find your assignments

  1. Click the course name on your CodeHS dashboard — Intro to Software Technology.
  2. Units and lessons are listed down the left side. Find the number that matches what's on this site (e.g. 2.1, 2.4, 2.10).
  3. Click an item to open it — notes/examples and assignments both live in that same list.

5. Do the work & know it's turned in

Most CodeHS assignments auto-save and auto-grade as you go — there's no separate "submit" step like Classroom. Just make sure you see a checkmark or "completed" status before moving on. Not sure something saved? Refresh the page and check that your code is still there.
