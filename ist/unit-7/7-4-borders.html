7.4 — Borders

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. A picture frame changes how a photo feels, even though the photo does not change. What does a border do for the content inside it on a webpage?
  2. A double border looks like an old certificate, and a dashed border looks like a coupon. What does a border style tell people before they read a single word?
  3. You can put a border on just the bottom of a heading, which works like an underline. When would that look better than a full box?
  4. If you write a border but forget the style word (solid, dashed...), the browser draws nothing. Why do you think it needs that word so badly?
