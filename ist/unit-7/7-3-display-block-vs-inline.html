3.10 — Display (Block vs Inline)

Very important — required to move any further in the unit. Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Block elements always force a new line, no matter what; inline elements sit side by side no matter what. Why would HTML hardcode that behavior into elements instead of just letting every tag act the same way?
  2. Today's notes call this "required to move any further in the unit." Why would something this basic-sounding — whether a box goes on its own line or not — be the one concept the whole rest of the unit depends on?
  3. If every element on a page were forced to be inline (nothing could ever start a new line), what would happen to a normal webpage with headings, paragraphs, and images?
  4. You can literally change any block element into inline, and any inline element into block, with one line of CSS. Does that mean the "block vs. inline" difference is really about the tag itself, or about a setting anyone can override?
