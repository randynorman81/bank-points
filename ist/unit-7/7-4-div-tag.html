3.11 — Div Tag

The most used, and most important, tag in the world. Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Today's notes call the div tag "the most used, and most important, tag in the world" — but a div does absolutely nothing visible on its own. How can the most important tag be one that has zero style by default?
  2. A div's entire job is just to group other elements together into one container. Why would grouping things — not displaying them, not styling them, just grouping — be such a powerful tool for organizing a page?
  3. If you removed every div tag from a huge, complicated website but kept every other tag exactly the same, what do you think would happen to how the page looks and behaves?
  4. You group your locker, your backpack, and your desk drawer even though the stuff inside is totally different. What's the real-world benefit of grouping unrelated things into one container, on a webpage or otherwise?
