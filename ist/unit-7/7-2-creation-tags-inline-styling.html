🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Pick a website you visit all the time. Name three things about how its text looks (color, size, alignment, capital letters). Could plain HTML alone do those things?
  2. Your 7.1 page was plain on purpose. If you could change only ONE thing about how it looks, what would it be? What do you think you would have to type to change it?
  3. Inline styling lets you style one single element directly inside its tag. Why might that convenience become a headache on a website with hundreds of pages?
  4. Predict: a style has a colon, a semicolon, and quotes. If you forgot the semicolon between two styles, what do you think the browser would do with the second one?
