7.8 — Box Model

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Every single element on every webpage is secretly a rectangular box, even things that look round or oddly shaped. Why would browsers force everything into the same rigid box shape behind the scenes?
  2. Padding pushes content away from the inside of a box; margin pushes the whole box away from other elements. If you mixed those two up while designing a page, what would actually go wrong visually?
  3. Two boxes can have the exact same content but completely different padding, border, and margin, making them totally different sizes on screen. Which do you think actually "defines" an element's size more — its content, or the space around it?
  4. A border takes up real space in the box model, just like padding does. Why do you think that surprises most people learning this for the first time?
