7.2 — Colors (Named Colors and Hex)

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. You could describe any color using a simple word like "red" or "blue," but developers usually use a 6-digit hex code instead. Why would something as precise as #FF0000 win out over words most people already understand?
  2. There are only 140 named colors in HTML, but millions of possible hex codes. What does that gap tell you about the limits of naming things with words versus naming things with numbers?
  3. Color choices on a website can change how trustworthy, cheap, exciting, or calm it feels before you even read a single word. Pick a brand you know well — what would change about how you feel about it if they swapped their main color?
  4. If two designers pick colors that are hex codes apart by just one digit, would anyone visiting the site actually notice the difference? At what point does precision in color stop mattering to a real human being?
