Notes

A card is a flexible content box — image on top, a title, some text, maybe a button — built entirely from classes on nested divs.

<div class="card"> <img src="photo.jpg" class="card-img-top" alt="A description"> <div class="card-body"> <h5 class="card-title">Card Title</h5> <p class="card-text">Some quick example text for the card.</p> <a href="#" class="btn btn-primary">Read More</a> </div> </div>
ClassWhat it does
cardThe outer border/box, on the outer <div>
card-img-topAn image that fills the card's width at the top
card-bodyPadding around everything below the image
card-title / card-textSpacing and sizing for the heading and paragraph inside

Put multiple cards inside col divs (from 9.2) to lay a whole row of them out at once — a card and the grid are almost always used together.

Look up these terms if anything above is unclear: card, card-body, card-title.
