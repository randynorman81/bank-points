9.1 — Homepage: Your First Website

Two class periods to complete both 9.1.3 and 9.1.4.

Directions

  1. Read the project info below — you're building an "entry level" website.
  2. Complete 9.1.3: set up your CodeHS domain.
  3. Complete 9.1.4: create your homepage.
  4. Submit both in your CodeHS sandbox.

Notes

This is a project day — no new vocab. Put together everything you've learned across the HTML unit (Unit 7) and the CSS unit (Unit 8) to build your own website from scratch.

Assignments

You will create an "entry level" website using the instructions in your CodeHS sandbox.

  • 9.1.3 Setting up your CodeHS domain (200 points, formative)
  • 9.1.4 Create your homepage (200 points, summative)

How much is it worth?

400 points total — 200 for setting up your CodeHS domain (9.1.3) and 200 for building your homepage (9.1.4).

Where do you turn it in?

CodeHS sandbox — both 9.1.3 and 9.1.4.
