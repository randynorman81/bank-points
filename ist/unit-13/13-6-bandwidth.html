9.6 — Bandwidth

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Your internet "speed" and your internet "bandwidth" are actually two related but different ideas. Based on the word itself, what do you think bandwidth is actually measuring?
  2. Netflix suddenly starts buffering the moment your sibling starts a huge download in another room. Nothing about your streaming device changed — so what did?
  3. A highway has a fixed number of lanes no matter how much traffic shows up. What do you think happens to how fast any one car can go once way more cars are on that highway at the same time?
  4. An ISP advertises "up to 500 Mbps," but a single phone streaming one video by itself couldn't possibly use all 500 Mbps at once. So who, or what, is that bandwidth actually for?
