9.1 — How the Internet Reaches Your House

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Water gets to your house through a giant network of pipes, from a treatment plant down to the one pipe that enters your kitchen. What do you think the "internet" version of that whole pipe network actually looks like?
  2. Your neighborhood almost certainly doesn't have its own private copy of every website in the world sitting nearby. So where is the data you're requesting actually coming from, physically?
  3. Rural areas often can't get fiber internet and rely on satellite instead. Why do you think laying cable to a house in the middle of nowhere is so much harder than to a house in a city?
  4. If a single undersea cable between continents gets damaged, it can slow down the internet for millions of people. What does that tell you about how "the internet" actually works underneath the Wi-Fi symbol on your phone?
