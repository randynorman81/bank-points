🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Search "what is my IP address" on two different phones connected to the SAME home Wi-Fi. Why do you think they'd show the exact same result, even though they're two different devices?
  2. Almost every home router in the world hands out an address like 192.168.1.5 to some device. If millions of houses are all using that same address, why doesn't the internet get confused about which one is which?
  3. An apartment building has one street address, but the mail carrier still gets each letter to the correct individual unit. What's doing that same job on your home network?
  4. Your phone's private IP address can change every time it reconnects to Wi-Fi, but a website you visit doesn't need to know or care about that. Why not?
