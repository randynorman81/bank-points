9.2 — Modems and Routers

Today's work is due by 11:59pm.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. Most homes today have one box that handles Wi-Fi, but it's technically doing two completely different jobs at once. What do you think those two jobs are?
  2. Your ISP only runs one wire (or one signal) into your house, but your family has a dozen devices online at the same time. How is that even possible with just one incoming connection?
  3. If your router restarts, your phone briefly loses Wi-Fi and then reconnects a few seconds later. What do you think happens behind the scenes during that gap?
  4. A modem and a router could theoretically be bought as two separate devices instead of one combo box. What would be the advantage of keeping them separate?

Assignment

In your own words, explain the two separate jobs a modem does and a router does, using the phrase "translator" for one and "traffic director" for the other. Then describe what would stop working in your house if only the modem failed, versus if only the router failed.
