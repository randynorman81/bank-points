🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. When a new phone joins your home Wi-Fi for the first time, nobody has to type in a private IP address for it by hand. Who's actually deciding what address it gets?
  2. If two devices on the same network accidentally had the exact same private IP address, what problems do you think that would cause?
  3. Hotels and coffee shops let hundreds of different guests' laptops join their Wi-Fi every single day without ever running out of available addresses. How is that possible if addresses aren't given out permanently?
  4. Your phone's private IP address can be different today than it was yesterday, even on the same home Wi-Fi. Why might that actually be a completely normal, expected thing?
