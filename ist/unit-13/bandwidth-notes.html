Bandwidth

Your internet plan isn't a promise of speed to every device — it's a promise of total road space, and everyone in the house has to share the same road.

bandwidth · Mbps · congestion · shared connection
01

The Highway, Not the Car

A highway has a fixed number of lanes no matter how much traffic shows up. With two cars on it, both can cruise at full speed. Cram fifty cars onto those same lanes, and every single one of them slows down — the road didn't get smaller, it just has to carry a lot more traffic. Your bandwidth is the highway. Every device streaming, downloading, or gaming is a car on it.

02

Bandwidth vs. Speed

Bandwidth

The total capacity of your connection, measured in Mbps or Gbps (megabits or gigabits per second). This is what your ISP plan actually sells you — the size of the whole highway.

Real-world speed

What one specific device actually experiences right now. It's usually less than the full bandwidth, because it's being split with every other active device — and with network congestion along the way.

"Up to 500 Mbps" was never a promise that one phone gets all 500.That number describes the whole household's shared road. A single phone playing one video rarely needs more than a small slice of it — the plan is sized for everyone streaming, gaming, and downloading at the same time.
