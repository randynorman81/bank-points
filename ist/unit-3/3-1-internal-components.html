3.1 — Internal Components

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. A GPU and a CPU can both "think," but companies build them completely differently on purpose. Why would a computer need two separate brains instead of one really powerful one?
  2. If you had to permanently give up either your computer's cooling system or its storage, which would you pick? What actually breaks first without each one?
  3. An SSD has no moving parts and an HDD does. Why do you think some people still buy HDDs at all if SSDs are faster and more reliable?
  4. The BIOS runs before your operating system even loads. Why does a computer need something to boot up the thing that boots everything else?
