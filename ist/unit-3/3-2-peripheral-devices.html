3.2 — Peripheral Devices

Covers ports, Bluetooth, and NFC in one lesson — one story-based assignment ties all three together.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Think About It
  1. USB-C is slowly replacing almost every other port. Why do you think it took decades for tech companies to finally agree on one universal shape?
  2. Tap-to-pay uses NFC, which only works from an inch or two away. Why would a payment company deliberately choose a "worse," shorter-range signal instead of Bluetooth?
  3. If Bluetooth and NFC can both send data wirelessly, why does your phone still need physical ports like USB-C at all? What could wireless never fully replace?
  4. Someone can "sniff" a Bluetooth pairing signal from across a room. Does that change how you feel about using tap-to-pay in a crowded place like a mall or a concert?
