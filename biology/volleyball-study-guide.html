A one-minute game-day review

  1. The season repeats: grow (G1), copy the playbook (S), check (G2), play the match (mitosis), then split the court (cytokinesis).
  2. In the match: warm up (prophase), line up at the net (metaphase), pull apart (anaphase), settle on your sides (telophase).
  3. Everyone on the court has the same playbook: genetic continuity.
  4. New teams come from two half-rosters joining: gametes (23 + 23) make a zygote (46).
  5. The gym door (cell membrane) is selective. Downhill moves are free (passive). Uphill moves cost energy (active).
