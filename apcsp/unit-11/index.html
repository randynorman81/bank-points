Unit 11 — Project: The Effects of the Internet

The Internet changed who can share ideas, work, and data. This unit is about what that change does to people: the good and bad effects, who gets left out, and how crowds solve problems. These pages cover Big Idea 5 on the AP exam (Impact of Computing).

Read the notes, try the activities, then open the lesson's walkthrough in the Python Editor. In the walkthrough you write what you learned as notes and test it with small programs.
