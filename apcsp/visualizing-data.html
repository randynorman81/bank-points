Why Visualize?

A table of 1,000 numbers hides the pattern that one picture shows in a second. A visualization turns data into a chart or graph so people can see patterns, trends, and outliers quickly and share a finding with others.

A good chart communicates. A bad one can mislead, even when every number in it is correct.

01

Choose the Right Chart

The best chart depends on the question.

ChartBest forExample
Linehow something changes over timea town's temperature each month
Barcomparing categoriesvotes for each lunch option
Pieparts of a whole (that add to 100%)how a budget is divided
Scatter plotthe relationship between two numbershours studied and test score
Histogramhow values are spread outhow many students scored in each 10-point range
02

Charts Can Mislead

Because people trust pictures, the way a chart is drawn changes what it seems to say.

Try it: the same two bars, two stories

Sales were 52 in Plan A and 55 in Plan B. That is only a 6% difference. Drag the bottom of the chart axis up and see what the picture claims.

TrickWhat it doesHow to spot it
Truncated axismakes a small difference look hugecheck where the axis starts
Cherry-picked rangeshows only the dates that tell one storyask what happened before and after
Missing labels or unitshides what is being measuredlook for a title, axis labels, and units
Different scales side by sidemakes two charts look comparable when they are notcompare the numbers, not the heights
03

Making a Chart Well

These habits make a visualization honest and easy to read.

  • Give it a title that says what it shows.
  • Label both axes and include units.
  • Start bar charts at zero.
  • Use color with care. Many people cannot tell red from green, so add labels or patterns.
  • Show all the data that matters. Do not hide the inconvenient part.

In Python, libraries like matplotlib turn a list of numbers into a chart. Using a library to draw charts is exactly the library idea from Unit 5: someone else wrote the plotting procedures, and you call them with the right arguments.

A line chart shows change over time.

Which chart best shows whether students who study more earn higher scores?
A scatter plot shows the relationship between two numeric values.

Choosing only convenient data misleads.
AP

On the AP Exam

These are the ideas the exam asks about. Be ready to explain each one in your own words.

  • Visualizations help people see patterns, trends, and outliers.
  • Match the chart to the question: line (time), bar (compare), pie (parts of a whole), scatter (relationship).
  • Charts can mislead: truncated axes, cherry-picked ranges, missing labels.
  • Libraries can draw charts from data, just as they provide other procedures.

Draw it honestly.
