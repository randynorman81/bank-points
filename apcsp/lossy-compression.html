Lossy Compression

Your eyes cannot see every tiny difference in color, and your ears cannot hear every tiny sound. Lossy compression throws away the details people barely notice, so files get much smaller. The price: what is thrown away cannot come back.

00

Lossy vs Lossless

Both make files smaller. They differ in one big way: can you get the original back?

LosslessLossy
Original back exactly?YesNo
File sizesmallermuch smaller
Qualityperfectslightly worse (you may not notice)
ExamplesZIP, PNG, text filesJPEG photos, MP3 music, streaming video

Fewer shades need fewer bits per pixel, so the file shrinks. But once two shades are merged into one, you can never tell which of them was the original.
02

Ways to Lose Data

Different kinds of data lose different things.

DataWhat lossy compression drops
Photos (JPEG)tiny color differences your eye barely notices, and fine detail
Music (MP3)very quiet sounds hidden by louder ones, and sounds too high or low to hear well
Videodetail that does not change between frames, and fine detail in fast motion
Any imageresolution (fewer pixels) or colors (fewer shades)
03

Python: Merging Values

Here is lossy compression in two lines. Every value is rounded down to a multiple of 64, so many different values become the same value.

values = [12, 70, 130, 200, 255] small = [] for v in values: small.append(v // 64 * 64) print(small) # [0, 64, 128, 192, 192]

Look at the end of the list. 200 and 255 both became 192. If you only have 192, you can never know which one it came from. That lost information is exactly what makes it lossy.

There is no uncompress function that could undo this. If you need the exact original, you must keep a copy of it before compressing lossily.
04

Which One Should I Use?

The right choice depends on what the data is for.

SituationBest choiceWhy
Sending a text document or a programLosslessone missing character could break it
Posting a vacation photoLossysmall file, nobody sees the difference
Streaming music or video over slow internetLossymust be small to send quickly
Storing a medical scan or an original photo you may edit laterLosslessevery detail may matter
The trade-offLossy: smaller file, lower quality, no going back. Lossless: bigger file, perfect copy.
05

On the AP Exam

These questions ask you to decide and to explain a trade-off.

  • Lossy compression cannot rebuild the exact original. Lossless can.
  • Lossy usually gives a smaller file than lossless.
  • Pick lossless when every bit matters and lossy when small size matters more than perfect detail.
  • A compressed file can be very different in size from the original with little visible change.

Let go of what nobody notices.
