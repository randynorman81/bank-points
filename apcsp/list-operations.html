Adding and Removing Items

A list can grow and shrink while the program runs. The AP exam has exactly four list operations to know: APPEND, INSERT, REMOVE, and LENGTH. This page shows what each one does to the list.

00

Watch a List Change

Start with a shopping cart. Click through four operations and watch what happens to the other items.

cart = ["milk", "eggs", "bread"] cart.append("jam") # adds to the END cart.insert(1, "rice") # adds at index 1, items after it move right cart.pop(3) # removes the item at index 3 (bread)

Click through the four lines

Start: a list with 3 items. len(cart) is 3.

cart.append("jam") adds jam at the end. Nothing else moves. The length goes from 3 to 4.

cart.insert(1, "rice") puts rice at index 1. Eggs, bread, and jam each move one place to the right. The length is 5.

cart.pop(3) removes the item at index 3, bread. Items after it move left to close the gap. The length is 4 again.

01

APPEND: Add to the End

append is the easiest and the most common. It always adds one item at the end, so no other item changes position.

scores = [] # an empty list scores.append(90) scores.append(85) print(scores) # [90, 85] print(len(scores)) # 2
Starting from an empty list and calling append inside a loop is how most programs collect data. You will use this pattern in your own Create Task.
02

INSERT: Add in the Middle

insert adds an item at a chosen position. Every item from that position onward shifts one place right to make room.

What you wantPythonAP exam pseudocode
put gold in position 2 (the second spot)colors.insert(1, "gold")INSERT(colors, 2, "gold")
put gold firstcolors.insert(0, "gold")INSERT(colors, 1, "gold")
Watch the index in INSERT. The exam counts from 1, Python counts from 0. INSERT(colors, 2, "gold") becomes colors.insert(1, "gold").
03

REMOVE: Take an Item Out

REMOVE deletes the item at a position. Every item after it shifts one place left, and the list gets one shorter.

What you wantPythonAP exam pseudocode
remove the item in position 2colors.pop(1)REMOVE(colors, 2)
remove the first itemcolors.pop(0)REMOVE(colors, 1)
how many items nowlen(colors)LENGTH(colors)
  • In Python, pop(index) removes the item at that index. (remove(value) removes the first item equal to a value, which is a different thing and is not on the exam.)
  • On the exam, REMOVE(list, index) removes by position, and the items after it shift left.
04

Practice Reading Exam Pseudocode

Work it out first, then click through. Remember the exam counts from 1.

fruit ← ["apple", "pear"] APPEND(fruit, "plum") INSERT(fruit, 1, "fig") REMOVE(fruit, 2) DISPLAY(fruit[2]) DISPLAY(LENGTH(fruit))

Trace it one line at a time

Line 1: the list has apple, pear.

APPEND adds plum at the end.

INSERT(fruit, 1, "fig") puts fig in position 1. Everything moves right.

REMOVE(fruit, 2) removes the item in position 2, which is apple. The rest move left.

DISPLAY(fruit[2]) shows pear. Then DISPLAY(LENGTH(fruit)) shows 3.

Append at the end, insert in the middle, remove by position.
