Every Innovation Has Two Sides

A computing innovation is a new program, device, or system that changes how people do things: social media, GPS, streaming, smart speakers. Almost every one has both beneficial and harmful effects, and often at the same time.

Try it: every innovation has two sides

Pick a computing innovation. See a benefit, a harm, an effect nobody planned, and who gets which.

The exam never asks "is technology good?" It asks you to name a specific effect and explain who it helps or harms and why.
01

Intended and Unintended Effects

The people who build an innovation have an intended purpose. But once millions of people use it, effects nobody planned appear.

Intended effectUnintended effect
What it iswhat the creators meant it to doa result nobody planned or predicted
Example: social medialet friends stay in touchchanged how people get news; spreads misinformation
Example: GPS in a phonegive directionsapps tracking your movements for ads
Can it be good?usuallyyes or no. Unintended does not mean bad.
Developers cannot predict every effect. That is why computing innovations are tested, studied, and sometimes changed after they are released.

03

How Computing Changes the World

Computing has made huge changes in creativity, communication, business, health, and education.

1

Creativity

Anyone can make and share music, video, and art, and remix other people's work to create something new.

2

Communication

People can talk instantly across the world, but also spread rumors just as fast.

3

Health

Phone apps and sensors track health, and doctors share records. Data can also be leaked or misused.

4

Jobs

New jobs appear (app developers, data analysts) while others are automated away.

Check yourself

Answer each one. You see why right away, so read the explanation even when you are right.

A fitness app was built to help people track their runs. It also publicly showed where people ran, which revealed the location of private places. What kind of effect is this?

The creators never intended to show private places, and it caused harm. That is an unintended harmful effect.

Which statement is most accurate about computing innovations?

Most innovations have both kinds of effects, and many effects are unpredicted.

A store replaces most cashiers with self-checkout kiosks. Which is a harmful effect?

The others are benefits. Job loss is a harm to a particular group.

Which is an example of an unintended beneficial effect?

Nobody planned for the cooking site to become a repair school, but it helps people. That is an unplanned benefit.
AP

On the AP Exam

These are the ideas the exam asks about. Be ready to explain each one in your own words.

  • Computing innovations can have both beneficial and harmful effects, sometimes at the same time.
  • Effects can be intended (what the creators planned) or unintended. Unintended does not always mean harmful.
  • An effect can help one group and harm another. Name the group.
  • Developers cannot always predict the effects of what they create.
  • Computing has changed creativity, communication, business, health, and jobs.

Name the effect, name the group, name the why.
